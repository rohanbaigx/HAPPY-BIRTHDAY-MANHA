MERI PYAARI BEGUM MANHA… ❤️

meri zindagi ka sabse khoobsurat hissa,

meri muskurahat ki wajah,

aur meri har dua ka naam…

Tere bina,

sab kuch adhura sa lagta hai

jaise din ho lekin roshni na ho,

jaise saans ho lekin zindagi na ho…

tu meri aadat ban chuki hai,

woh aadat jo kabhi chori nahi ja sakti

Kabhi kabhi sochta hoon

kaise itni important ban gayi tu meri life mein,

ke ab har khushi tujhse judi lagti hai,

aur har gham tere saath halka ho jata hai…

Main perfect nahi hoon,yeh mujhe pata hai…

kabhi gussa, kabhi pagal, kabhi over,

tu phir bhi bardasht krti akela nahi chorti

Teri ek smile, mera pura din bana deti hai…

aur tera udaas hona,mujhe andar se tod deta hai…

Tu woh insan hai jo kismat walon ko milta hai,

aur mujhe fakhar hai tu meri zindagi ka hissa hai…

jab tak main hoon tu kabhi akeli nahi hogi ❤️

Har situation mein, har waqt,

main tere saath hoon…

main tujhe kabhi khona nahi chahta

Tere is khaas din par,

main sirf yeh dua karta hoon…

Allah tujhe hamesha khush rakhe,

teri har dua qabool ho,

aur teri zindagi hamesha pyaar se bhari rahe… 🌙

Or hamari jaldi se shaadi hojaye

Happy Birthday once again meri jaan… 💖
